3 Signs

Work. Expands / by the gas → W +. Compressed / on the gas → W −.

Heat. In → Q +. Out → Q −.

Entropy = a wallet of heat. Gets heat → ΔS +. Gives heat → ΔS −. Same heat, opposite signs for giver and receiver.

Hot or cold? Look at the reservoir the sentence names: "from the hot reservoir" → Q_H, "to/from the cold reservoir" → Q_C. Q_H is always the biggest.

4 Units

5 Heat · P1–39

$$Q=mc\Delta T,\qquad Q=mL,\qquad Q_{\text{lost}}=Q_{\text{gained}}$$
$$\Delta L=\alpha L_0\Delta T,\qquad \Delta A=2\alpha A_0\Delta T,\qquad \Delta V=3\alpha V_0\Delta T$$
$$x=\sqrt{\left(\frac{L'}{2}\right)^2-\left(\frac{L}{2}\right)^2},\qquad L'=L(1+\alpha\Delta T)$$
$$H=\frac{Q}{t}=\frac{kA\Delta T}{d},\qquad E=Pt,\qquad mgh=mc\Delta T,\qquad \frac{1}{2}mv^2=mL,\qquad m=\rho V$$

6 Gases · P40–76

$$pV=nRT,\qquad n=\frac{m}{M},\qquad \frac{p_1V_1}{T_1}=\frac{p_2V_2}{T_2}$$

Cross out what is constant. Constant p → V/T. Constant V → p/T. Constant T → pV.

$$v_{rms}=\sqrt{\frac{3RT}{M}},\qquad \bar K=\frac{3}{2}k_BT,\qquad K=\frac{3}{2}nRT$$
$$N=nN_A,\qquad pV=Nk_BT,\qquad m_{\text{molecule}}=\frac{M}{N_A}$$
$$p=p_0+\rho gh,\qquad M_{\text{mix}}=\sum x_iM_i,\qquad \rho=\frac{pM}{RT}$$

7 1st law · P77–114

$$\Delta U=Q-W,\qquad \Delta U=\frac{3}{2}nR\Delta T=\frac{3}{2}(p_2V_2-p_1V_1)$$
$$W=p\Delta V\ \text{(const p)},\qquad W=\text{area under the path}$$
$$W=nRT\ln\frac{V_2}{V_1}\ \text{(const T)}$$
$$Q=nC\Delta T,\qquad C_p-C_V=R,\qquad p\Delta V=nR\Delta T$$
$$TV^{\gamma-1}=\text{const},\qquad pV^{\gamma}=\text{const}\ \text{(adiabatic)}$$

Halved volume. T × 2^0.4, p × 2^1.4. p gets the full γ, T gets one less.

8 2nd law · P115–150

$$e=\frac{W}{Q_H},\qquad Q_H=W+Q_C,\qquad e_{\text{Carnot}}=1-\frac{T_C}{T_H}$$
$$K=\frac{Q_C}{W}=\frac{T_C}{T_H-T_C}\ \text{(fridge)},\qquad e=\frac{P_{out}}{P_{in}}$$
$$\Delta S=\frac{Q}{T}\ \text{(T fixed)},\qquad \Delta S=mc\ln\frac{T_2}{T_1}\ \text{(T changes)}$$

T fixed = lake, melting (273 K), boiling or condensing (373 K), isothermal. T changes = something heating or cooling.

Universe / total = add every part's ΔS. Always positive.
