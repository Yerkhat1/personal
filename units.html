Units, fast

Three rules, one table, then drill until it's automatic.

1 Three rules

Convert first. Before you plug in, everything goes to m, kg, s. Then the answer comes out in SI by itself.

Unit = what they ask. Asked for force → N. Asked for speed → m/s. Read the last sentence of the problem.

Ratio = no unit. "By what factor", "how many times", "ratio" → leave the unit box empty.
